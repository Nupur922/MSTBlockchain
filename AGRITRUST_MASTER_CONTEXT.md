## 📜 3. OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT

> **The Real-World Crisis**:  
> In agricultural regions across India—such as the Brahmaputra flood basin in Assam and the Kosi river basin in Bihar—smallholder farmers lose over **$100 Billion annually** to catastrophic monsoon floods and droughts. Traditional government and private crop insurance schemes (such as PMFBY) take **3 to 6 months** to issue relief payouts because they rely on manual paper assessments, corrupt human field adjusters who demand bribes, and slow bureaucratic treasuries. Farmers default on loans and fall into severe debt traps long before payout checks arrive.
>
> **The AgriTrust AI Solution**:  
> **AgriTrust AI** is an autonomous, satellite-driven parametric crop insurance platform on **MST Blockchain Layer 1** powered by **NEWRRO AI Remote Sensing**. It replaces manual human adjusters with **Sentinel-1 SAR Radar Satellite Data** (which penetrates heavy monsoon storm clouds), **NEWRRO AI Multi-Source Oracle Consensus**, and **MST Layer 1 Smart Contract Escrow Payouts in 2 Seconds** directly to the farmer's wallet.

## 🌾 5. ZERO-FRICTION GRASSROOTS MODEL (Illiterate Farmer Adoption)

To address rural illiteracy and lack of tech access:

1. **Zero Typing Onboarding**: Village *Krishi Mitras* or Common Service Center (CSC) operators scan government land record QR codes (*Bihar Bhumi / Bhu-Naksha*). The app automatically extracts farm polygon coordinates.
2. **Aadhaar-Linked Account Abstraction**: MST Blockchain wallets are generated automatically linked to the farmer's Aadhaar & mobile number—zero passwords or 12-word seed phrases to manage.
3. **Regional Language Voice Calls**: Farmers receive automated Voice Calls / WhatsApp Voice Notes in their native dialect (Assamese, Bhojpuri, Hindi):
   > 🔊 *"Ram Singh Ji, satellite radar confirmed 6 days of flood damage on your plot in Darbhanga. ₹25,000 relief payout has been sent to your Aadhaar bank account!"*
4. **AePS Fingerprint Cash Withdrawal**: The MST payout converts automatically to INR via AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System). Farmers withdraw cash at their local Village Post Office using a simple fingerprint scan.

## 🔒 7. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE

1. **Reentrancy Protection**: OpenZeppelin `ReentrancyGuard` ensures state updates occur *before* external token transfers.
2. **Cryptographic Proof Verification**: `AgriTrustVault.sol` verifies off-chain telemetry proofs signed by NEWRRO AI Agent using `ECDSA.recover`.
3. **Signature Replay Prevention**: `mapping(bytes32 => bool) public executedProofs` reverts double-submission of identical proof hashes.
4. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Restricted permissions managed via OpenZeppelin `AccessControl`.
5. **Multi-Source Oracle Consensus**: Requires 2-out-of-3 agreement between Sentinel-1 SAR Radar, Sentinel-2 NDVI, and IMD Weather APIs.
